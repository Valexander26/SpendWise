The problem

Your bank app knows the shop. It does not know the basket.

A bank statement can show Tesco, Boots, Amazon, or Greggs. But it cannot tell you whether that spend was food, toiletries, repeat items, impulse buys, returns you forgot about, or patterns hiding inside the receipt.

What you get from a bank app

  • ×One transaction total with no product detail.
  • ×No item-level memory of what you actually bought.
  • ×No return deadline reminders from your receipts.
  • ×Often needs Open Banking or account connection.

What SpendWise adds

  • ✓Receipt scanning that extracts real items and prices.
  • ✓Spending insights built from what was inside the basket.
  • ✓Return deadline tracking before money is lost.
  • ✓No bank login, no credential anxiety, no broken connections.
Built differently

The app your receipts have been waiting for.

SpendWise is designed for people who already shop like normal — they just want the receipt to finally do something useful afterwards.

📸

Scan receipts in seconds

Use the camera or gallery to capture a receipt and turn it into organised spending data with item names, prices, categories, and store detail.

🧾

See what you actually bought, not just where your money went. Perfect for spotting repeat purchases, rising basket costs, and sneaky categories.

Questions

Quick answers before you join.

Does SpendWise connect to my bank?

No. SpendWise is built around receipts, so you do not need to connect a bank account or share banking credentials.

Is SpendWise only for Android?

The current launch focus is Android and Google Play testing. The codebase is planned with future iOS support in mind.

What makes it different from budgeting apps?

Most budgeting apps categorise transactions. SpendWise focuses on receipt-level detail — the products, prices, returns, and patterns inside the basket.

Who should become a beta tester?

Anyone in the UK who shops regularly, can install the Android app, and is willing to give honest feedback before public launch.
